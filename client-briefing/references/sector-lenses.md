# Sector lenses

## Banking
Bias the briefing toward:
- regulatory pressure
- core transformation
- risk / finance / treasury / payments
- data, AI, and operating model modernization

## Insurance
Bias the briefing toward:
- Solvency II / IFRS 17 / actuarial context
- claims / underwriting / distribution transformation
- operating efficiency and automation
- InsurTech or ecosystem signals

## Wealth & Asset Management
Bias the briefing toward:
- portfolio and advisory platforms
- client reporting and digital channels
- MiFID / ESG / suitability pressure
- consolidation and operating leverage
