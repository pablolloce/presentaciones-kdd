# Briefing structure

Use this structure by default unless the user asks for a shorter or more specific format.

## Header
- client name
- prepared date
- meeting date/purpose if known
- prepared for / by if useful

## 1. Company snapshot
- who they are
- scale and business model
- financial size indicators if relevant
- position in the market

## 2. Leadership and decision makers
- CEO / relevant C-level
- business owner relevant to the meeting
- known sponsor or key contact if provided
- relevant recent leadership changes

## 3. Strategic priorities
- current plan, transformation, or agenda
- public statements that matter
- growth / cost / regulatory / AI priorities

## 4. Technology and operating context
- core platforms if public
- data / cloud / AI signals
- major vendors, integrators, or architecture changes

## 5. Regulatory and risk context
- important regulatory pressure, remediation, or risk themes
- fines, audit findings, or public supervisory themes if relevant

## 6. Recent news
- 5-8 relevant items from the last 6 months when available
- each item should say why it matters

## 7. nfq angle
- likely pain points
- how nfq could help
- conversation hooks
- specific value propositions or questions to ask

## 8. Talking points
- 3-5 concrete lines for the meeting
- format: topic -> why it matters -> nfq angle
