# Offering deck structure

## 1. Cover
- title for the vertical or capability area
- subtitle / year if useful

## 2. Why this matters / context
- why the vertical or problem matters now
- what clients are dealing with

## 3. About nfq
- scale, presence, service lines, relevant positioning
- keep it credible and concise

## 4. Domains / scope
- what areas are covered in this offering
- organize clearly by domain or function

## 5. Capabilities
- core capability grid
- accelerators / products / AI enablers where relevant

## 6. Services / delivery model
- advisory
- tech & data
- operations / managed services if relevant

## 7. Why nfq / differentiation
- 3-5 clear differentiators
- proof points over slogans

## 8. Annex
- deeper vertical pages
- relevant credentials
- additional capability detail

## Working rule
Outline first. Full copy second. Pretty HTML third.
