Create offering decks that explain who nfq is, what it does, and why the client should care.

## Workflow
1. Clarify vertical, audience, and whether this is a general capabilities deck or a focused offering.
2. Read `references/structure.md` before drafting.
3. Build the outline first; do not jump straight into full deck copy.
4. Gather verified proof points, capabilities, and differentiators.
5. Draft in markdown first unless the user explicitly asks for the final artifact immediately.
6. If a premium HTML deck is needed, use `html-presentation` + `frontend-design`.

## Quality bar
- Strong storyline, not a pile of capability bullets.
- Specific proof points and concrete examples.
- Clear differentiation versus generic consulting language.
- Adapt detail level to audience: executive vs delivery vs procurement.

## Guardrails
- Use verified nfq figures only.
- Mark assumptions clearly.
- Keep the deck as an internal draft unless the user clearly wants a client-facing version.
- If the user really wants opportunity mapping before the deck, `value-studio` may be the better first tool.
