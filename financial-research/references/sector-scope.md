# Sector scope

## Banking
Common domains:
- treasury / liquidity / ALM
- market, credit, operational risk
- regulatory reporting
- payments
- lending / commercial banking
- CIB / wealth / corporate functions

## Insurance
Common domains:
- underwriting
- claims
- actuarial / reserving
- IFRS 17 / Solvency II
- distribution
- operating model / automation

## Wealth & Asset Management
Common domains:
- advisory and portfolio management
- fund operations and custody
- client reporting
- ESG / suitability / MiFID pressure
- digital channels and operating efficiency

## Cross-cutting themes
- AI / ML in financial services
- cloud and architecture modernization
- data governance
- Open Finance / digital channels
- cybersecurity
- cost transformation
