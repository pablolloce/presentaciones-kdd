# Research structure

## Executive summary
- 3-5 key takeaways
- one sentence on why it matters for nfq or the target client

## 1. Market context
- size, trends, adoption, major players, important moves

## 2. Regulatory landscape
- current regulation in force
- upcoming regulation / consultation / deadlines
- cross-jurisdiction differences if material

## 3. Technology and innovation
- relevant solutions, vendors, architecture patterns, AI use cases

## 4. Key challenges
- operational, regulatory, data, or delivery frictions

## 5. Best practices and benchmarks
- what better institutions or operators are doing differently
- named examples when possible

## 6. Implications for nfq
- where the opportunity is
- which capabilities or products matter
- what the next action should be

## 7. Sources
- list the most important sources clearly
