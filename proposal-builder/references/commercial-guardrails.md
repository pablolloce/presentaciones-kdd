# Commercial guardrails

## Required before pricing
- clear scope or explicitly bounded assumptions
- duration hypothesis
- team shape hypothesis
- delivery model hypothesis

## Rules
- use verified nfq figures only
- never invent case studies
- never imply commitment that has not been approved
- prefer ranges or scenarios when uncertainty is high
- distinguish binding proposal vs exploratory estimate

## Review gates
Treat proposal output as internal draft until Gregor reviews:
- scope
- methodology
- commercials
- proof points

## When to stop and flag
Stop and call out the issue if:
- the scope is too fuzzy to price honestly
- the user is asking for fixed numbers without enough basis
- credentials or references are missing
- timeline is clearly unrealistic
