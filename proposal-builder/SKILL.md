Draft proposals that are commercially useful, structurally sound, and honest about assumptions.

## Workflow
1. Clarify client, engagement type, topic, duration, and known constraints.
2. Read `references/structure.md` before drafting.
3. Read `references/commercial-guardrails.md` before writing pricing, team, or assumptions.
4. Start with an outline and assumptions list.
5. Draft in markdown first by default.
6. Only move to a premium HTML deck when explicitly requested or clearly needed; in that case, use `html-presentation` + `frontend-design`.

## Quality bar
- Demonstrate real understanding of the client situation.
- Separate facts, assumptions, and proposed approach.
- Make methodology, governance, deliverables, and commercials legible.
- Keep tone partner-like and specific, not inflated.

## Guardrails
- Never invent credentials, case studies, or pricing certainty.
- Explicitly flag assumptions, exclusions, and dependencies.
- Treat all proposal outputs as internal draft material until reviewed.
- If pricing is weakly specified, provide ranges or scenarios instead of fake precision.
