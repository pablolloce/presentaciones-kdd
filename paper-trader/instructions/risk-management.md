# Risk Management Rules (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

1. **Max 15% of portfolio per position** — at time of purchase
2. **Max 5 simultaneous positions**
3. **Stop-loss at -10%** — if current_price ≤ avg_price × 0.90, SELL ALL. No "it'll come back."
4. **Take-profit review at +20%** — don't auto-sell, but re-evaluate thesis. Trim if thesis weakening.
5. **Long only** — no shorts, no options, no leverage, no margin
6. **US equities only** — NYSE/NASDAQ listed
7. **No penny stocks** — minimum $1B market cap
8. **No FOMO** — never chase a stock that's already up >10% in a day without a thesis

## Position Sizing
- Default position: 10% of portfolio value
- High conviction: up to 15% (hard max)
- Calculate shares: floor(allocation / price)
- Always keep ≥10% cash reserve
