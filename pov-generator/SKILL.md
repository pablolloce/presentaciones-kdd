Create POV documents that position nfq as thoughtful and credible, not as a brochure with a thesis stapled on top.

## Workflow
1. Clarify topic, audience, geography, and target depth.
2. Use `financial-research` first if the factual substrate is still weak.
3. Read `references/structure.md` before drafting.
4. Build the thesis and outline first.
5. Draft in markdown by default.
6. Only build premium HTML when explicitly requested or when a shareable thought-leadership artifact is clearly needed; if so, use `html-presentation` + `frontend-design`.

## Quality bar
- Strong problem framing before solutioning.
- Specific regulation / market evidence when relevant.
- A real nfq point of view, not consensus paraphrasing.
- Clear implications and next moves.

## Guardrails
- Never invent sources, regulation numbers, or benchmarks.
- Mark opinion vs evidence clearly.
- Keep client-facing thought leadership under human review.
- If the piece turns into a sales deck, pivot to `offering-deck` or `proposal-builder`.
