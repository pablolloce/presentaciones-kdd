# Humanization Quality Checklist

Run these checks against the final output before delivering.

## Pass/Fail Criteria

### Content quality
- [ ] No inflated significance claims (pattern 1)
- [ ] No vague attributions — sources are specific (pattern 5)
- [ ] No formulaic "challenges and future prospects" sections (pattern 6)
- [ ] Specific details replace vague claims throughout

### Language quality
- [ ] No high-frequency AI vocabulary words (pattern 7)
- [ ] Simple copulas (is/are/has) used where appropriate (pattern 8)
- [ ] No "not only...but also" or "it's not just...it's..." constructions (pattern 9)
- [ ] No forced groups of three (pattern 10)
- [ ] Consistent terminology — no synonym cycling (pattern 11)

### Style quality
- [ ] Em dashes used sparingly (max 1-2 per page) (pattern 13)
- [ ] Boldface used for genuine emphasis, not decoration (pattern 14)
- [ ] No emoji-decorated headings or bullets (pattern 17)
- [ ] Straight quotes, not curly (pattern 18)

### Voice quality
- [ ] Sentence length varies naturally
- [ ] Text sounds natural when read aloud
- [ ] Has opinions or perspective where appropriate
- [ ] Acknowledges complexity or uncertainty where relevant
- [ ] No chatbot artifacts (pattern 19)
- [ ] No sycophantic tone (pattern 21)
- [ ] No generic positive conclusion (pattern 24)
