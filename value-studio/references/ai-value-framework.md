# Bessemer AI Value Framework

Reference framework for pricing AI solutions. Use when scoring use cases in Phase 3 and positioning pricing in Phase 5.

Source: Bessemer Venture Partners, "AI Value Framework" (2024-2026)

## Pricing Power Spectrum

| Level | Value Type | Description | Pricing Model | Power |
|-------|-----------|-------------|---------------|-------|
| **1. AI-Enhanced** | Efficiency | AI augments existing workflows (autocomplete, summarization, copilots) | Traditional SaaS per-seat + AI premium | Low-Medium |
| **2. AI-Native** | Capability | Product rebuilt around AI; delivers capabilities impossible without it | Consumption-based or outcome-adjacent | Medium-High |
| **3. AI-Automated** | Labor substitution | AI replaces a defined human task end-to-end | Per-task, per-workflow, or per-outcome | High |
| **4. AI-Autonomous** | Full autonomy | AI agent operates independently, replacing headcount | Outcome-based or labor-equivalent pricing (% of salary replaced) | Very High |

**Key insight:** As you move from Level 1→4, pricing shifts from cost-plus to value-based. The further right, the more you charge based on outcomes delivered rather than software consumed.

## Scoring Dimensions

Evaluate each AI solution along:

1. **Determinism of output** — Is the AI output predictable? Higher determinism = easier outcome pricing
2. **Human-in-the-loop dependency** — How much oversight required? Less human = higher pricing power
3. **Measurability of value** — Can you quantify ROI? Clearer measurement = stronger pricing justification
4. **Workflow completeness** — Does the AI handle the full task or just a step? Full task = higher pricing
5. **Switching cost** — How deeply embedded does the AI become? Higher lock-in = more pricing leverage

## NFQ Application

When building value propositions for NFQ AI solutions:

### Alquid (ALM platform)
- Level 3-4: Automated IRRBB calculations, autonomous scenario generation
- Price on: outcomes (regulatory reports produced, scenarios analyzed) not seats

### AI-powered advisory
- Level 2-3: AI-native analysis that couldn't exist without AI (cross-regulation synthesis, real-time risk monitoring)
- Price on: deliverables and insights, not hours

### Positioning language
- Don't say "AI-enhanced consulting" (Level 1 = commodity)
- Say "autonomous regulatory intelligence" (Level 4 = premium)
- Quantify labor replacement: "replaces 3 FTEs of manual IRRBB reporting"

## Founder Best Practices (adapted for NFQ)

1. **Price on the metric the buyer already tracks** — regulatory fines avoided, FTE hours saved, time-to-report
2. **Start with outcome pricing, retreat to consumption** — if the client pushes back on outcome pricing, consumption (per-report, per-scenario) is the fallback, never per-seat
3. **Build the ROI calculator into the sales process** — show concrete $ impact before discussing price
4. **Charge for accuracy, not access** — if your AI is more accurate, that's the premium differentiator
5. **Create usage-based expansion** — start with one use case, expand to adjacent workflows
